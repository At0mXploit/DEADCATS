# DEADCATS Research

**DEADCATS** is a cybersecurity research team from Nepal focused on offensive security, reverse engineering, digital forensics, and vulnerability research.

This site is our public research space. It brings together technical blogs, exploit development notes, reverse engineering case studies, and applied security analysis in a format that is meant to be readable, useful, and professional.

## Editorial Approach

We aim to keep these research notes direct, reproducible, and technically useful. The goal is to document the reasoning, the failure points, and the attack path clearly enough that another researcher can follow or extend the work.
